# GridPass Engine - Feature List

## 1. Parts Management
- **Inventory Tracking:** Real-time stock levels for race parts (consumables, spares, high-value components).
- **Service Life Monitoring:** Track mileage/heat cycles on critical parts (engines, gearboxes, brakes).
- **QR/Barcode Integration:** Quick scanning for part check-out/check-in during race weekends.
- **Supplier Directory:** Integrated contact info for specialty restoration and racing parts.

## 2. Racing Telemetry & Data
- **Live Stream Integration:** Pulling telemetry data from standard racing protocols (CAN bus, OBD-II).
- **Session Logging:** Track times, weather conditions, and tire pressures/temps for every outing.
- **Driver Performance Portal:** Side-by-side lap analysis and video overlay capabilities.
- **Maintenance Triggers:** Automated alerts based on telemetry data (e.g., "Brake fluid flush needed after 50 laps").

## 3. Restoration Timeline (The Werk Shop Sync)
- **Concours Build Progress:** High-fidelity photo gallery and milestone tracking for long-term restoration projects.
- **Client Transparency:** Real-time budget tracking and approval workflow for new parts/labor.
- **History Documentation:** Digital "Birth Certificate" and restoration log for vintage BMWs.
