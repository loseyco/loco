## 1. UI/UX Improvements

### 🎨 Visual & Aesthetic Enhancements
*   **Consistency in Naming:** The site mentions "Founding 50" on some checkout buttons but "Founding 100" in the headers and changelog. Standardize this to avoid user confusion.
*   **Interactive "Coming Soon" States:** The "Coming Soon" modules (Shop OS, My Garage, etc.) should have "Notify Me" or "Join Waitlist" buttons to capture lead intent before the features launch.
*   **Dynamic Backgrounds:** While the static racing images are high quality, adding subtle parallax or slow-zoom effects would enhance the "premium" feel mentioned in the brand identity.
*   **Resume Builder Visibility:** Since the Resume Builder is the most mature feature, it should have a dedicated menu item or a more prominent "Try it Now" section on the home page, rather than just being one of the platform capability cards.

### 🗺️ Navigation & Flow
*   **Onboarding Checklist:** For new users, a "Pit Stop" onboarding flow (e.g., 1. Build Profile, 2. Add Career History, 3. Get Verified) would increase retention.
*   **Search Functionality:** As the "Members Directory" grows, a robust search bar for specific specialties (e.g., "IndyCar Engineer", "MoTeC Specialist") is essential.

## 2. Monetization Strategies

### 📈 Short-Term (Founder Era)
*   **Tiered Founder Passes:** Introduce "Silver" and "Gold" tiers. Silver ($4) for individuals, Gold ($49) for small teams/shops with advanced storage.
*   **Add-on: Verified Pro Badges:** A one-time fee for manual verification of racing credentials (ID check + career verification) to add a "Pro" checkmark.

### 🚀 Long-Term (The "A La Carte" Model)
*   **Usage-Based Storage:** Charge for high-resolution photo/video storage in "My Garage" or large telemetry file uploads in "Shop OS".
*   **Team Seat Subscriptions:** Shift from single-user lifetime access to per-seat monthly billing for "Race Teams" and "Command" modules.
*   **Recruitment Fee / "Hire Me" Commission:** If a team hires a member via the "Hire Me" button, GridPass could facilitate the contract and take a small platform fee (escrow service).
*   **Marketplace Featured Listings:** Charge for "Top of the Grid" placement in the upcoming Classifieds section.

## 3. Activity Tracking (Project Page)

### 📊 Modernizing the Dashboard
To better track activities, especially for complex projects like `0579f43a-0e87-40f7-9ccb-627a700833a5`, the following should be implemented:

*   **Project Telemetry Feed:** A real-time, scrolling activity log (similar to a code commit history) showing every action taken by the "Project Manager AI" or human contributors.
*   **AI Pulse Summary:** A daily "Project Pulse" generated by the AI that summarizes what happened in the last 24 hours (e.g., "AI updated 3 SEO metadata tags", "2 new experts joined the project").
*   **Gantt/Timeline View:** A visual timeline for the specific project ID, showing past milestones and future scheduled tasks.
*   **Activity Heatmap:** A GitHub-style contribution calendar specifically for that project to visualize team engagement levels.
*   **System Health Indicators:** If the project involves automated agents, show "Heartbeat" statuses (Live, Idle, Error) for each agent assigned to the project.
